Core orientation

CORE

Start with the direction words. A tropism is not an animal-like movement: it is a directional growth response to an environmental stimulus.

A

Tropism

Growth directed towards or away from a stimulus.

positive → towardnegative ← away
B

Phototropism

Growth towards or away from the direction of light.

shoot: positiveroot: mostly negative
C

Gravitropism

Growth towards or away from Earth’s gravitational pull.

root: positiveshoot: negative
↗

Key point. Tropic responses rely on differential cell elongation, so the response is a growth change and is effectively irreversible — not a rapid, reversible muscular movement.

The whole-plant pattern

Two organs, four directions

Positive and negative responses work together. Shoots lift leaves into light to support photosynthesis; roots anchor the plant and explore soil for water and mineral ions.

SHOOTlight ↑ positive phototropismgravity ↑ negative gravitropism
ROOTlight ↓ negative phototropismgravity ↓ positive gravitropism

Investigate the response

CORE

A strong practical does not just show a bend. It isolates the stimulus, measures the response and keeps other conditions constant.

SESSION A · CORE 3

Phototropism setup

Use identical seedlings. Compare overhead light, one-sided light and darkness. Record direction and angle of shoot curvature.

  • Overhead light → shoot grows straight.
  • One-sided light → shoot bends towards the light.
  • Darkness → shoot grows tall without directional bending.
CHECK 02ARead the experiment

Why does the shoot in Box B bend towards the light?

SESSION B · CORE 3

Testing gravitropism

Place a growing seedling horizontally. A stationary seedling curves: the shoot up and the root down. A slowly rotating clinostat reduces directional curvature because gravity acts on all sides over time.

Do not say: the clinostat removes gravity. It reduces a consistent one-sided stimulus.
INDEPENDENTLight direction, or stationary vs rotating.
DEPENDENTAngle of curvature or height difference across flanks.
CONTROLLEDSpecies, age, temperature, moisture and nutrients.
CORE 3 · SEED ORIENTATION

Gravity sets the direction — not the starting position.

Plant seeds upside down or on their sides in total darkness. The emerging radicle bends downwards and the plumule bends upwards, regardless of the seed’s original orientation. This is evidence for gravitropism.

03 / 08

Auxin: the chemical signal

EXTENDED

Plants have no nerves or muscles. Their coordination depends on chemical control. Auxin is made at the apex, then acts below it where cells expand.

01
Synthesis

Auxin (for example, IAA) is made in the shoot tip, the apical meristem.

02
Transport

It diffuses downwards from the apex into the sub-apical elongation zone.

03
Action

In stems, a higher auxin level loosens the wall so turgor pressure can stretch the cell.

KEY IDEAMade at the apex.
Acts below the apex.

Normal auxin levels

Cellulose microfibrils retain rigid structural bonding. The cell stays compact and growth proceeds at a baseline rate.

Elevated auxin levels

Expansin proteins loosen cellulose bonds. Turgor pressure stretches the wall, elongating the cell.

CHECK 03Trace the signal

Cells on the shaded flank are 2–3× longer than cells on the lit flank, while cell division rates are similar. What does this evidence support?

04 / 08

Mechanism: from stimulus to curve

EXTENDED

The visible curve is a mechanical result of unequal expansion. Learn the sequence once, then use it to explain both light and gravity responses.

Phototropism · EXTENDED 4–5(c–d)

Light shifts the distribution of intact auxin.

Unilateral light does not simply destroy auxin on the illuminated side. Instead, auxin is redistributed laterally across the tip towards the shaded side, then diffuses down that flank.

01light from one side
02auxin migrates to shade
03shaded cells elongate
04shoot bends to light
Shoot gravitropism · EXTENDED 5(d)

Gravity also creates an auxin gradient.

In a horizontal shoot, auxin accumulates on the lower side. Because auxin stimulates elongation in shoots, lower-side cells grow longer and the shoot bends upward — away from gravity.

ROOT · extrahigh auxin → inhibits elongation → bends down

05 / 08

Evidence that made the model

EXTENDED

The auxin model is not a guess. A chain of classic experiments tested where the stimulus is sensed, how a signal travels and whether that signal is chemical.

COLEOPTILES

Is the tip essential?

Intact tips curve. Decapitated tips do not. An opaque cap blocks curvature, so the apex must sense unilateral light.

WENT’S AGAR BLOCK

Can the signal diffuse?

Excised tips release a water-soluble signal into agar. An off-centre agar block makes a decapitated shoot bend in darkness, proving a diffusible chemical can create unequal elongation.

∥
Mica sheet evidence

An impermeable mica barrier prevents curvature when placed on the shaded side, but not when placed on the lit side. The result supports directional signal movement through the shaded flank.

Cell evidence · EXTENDED 5(d)

It is expansion, not extra division.

Histological sections show shaded-flank epidermal cells stretched to roughly 2–3 times the length of lit-flank cells. The rate of cell division is not the cause of the bend; the elongation zone is.

2–3×longer cells on the shaded flank

06 / 08

Apply & challenge

EXTENDED

The same principles travel well beyond the standard diagram. Use the model to spot misconceptions, explain unusual environments and evaluate an unfamiliar setup.

MISCONCEPTION CHECK

“Sunlight destroys the auxin.”

False. Light triggers lateral transport of intact auxin molecules towards the shaded side. The gradient, not photodestruction, explains the bend.

SPACE BIOLOGY

What guides growth in microgravity?

Without a strong gravitational cue, roots may lose their orientation. Directional LED light can still guide growth: roots grow away from light while shoots grow towards it.

Beyond the stated syllabus

Auxin in practice

Auxins can encourage roots to form on cuttings in rooting powders. Synthetic auxins can also disrupt broad-leaved weeds. These are applications; the syllabus focus remains auxin’s effect on shoot growth.
